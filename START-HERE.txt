SceneTrace 3.0 for Windows x64

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted SceneTrace folder.
3. Double-click SceneTrace.exe. Keep _internal beside it.

Try Anime and search Pocket Monsters for official Pokémon TV episodes.
Try Universal Video Search and search Caminandes for an in-app video.
Use Video Sources > Add Video Website to connect another public site.
Use Memory Search when you remember scenes or plot details.

Settings > Test Archive HTTPS checks Internet Archive on your PC.
Choose Quit SceneTrace before replacing or upgrading the portable folder.
Your profile stays in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SceneTrace.

See docs/HELP.md for the step-by-step guide.
